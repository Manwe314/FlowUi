Detailed Description

Non-owning access to manager-owned single-line or chunked text.

The view is invalidated by the next mutation of its field. Single-line text is guaranteed contiguous. Multiline callers explicitly copy a range or visit its chunks, avoiding an accidental full-document flatten per frame.

Synchronously visit text chunks overlapping a byte range.

Bounds are clamped. Chunks are borrowed only during each call; copy to retain them and do not mutate the field while visiting. Visitor exceptions propagate unchanged; earlier visitor side effects remain visible.

Note
Makes no callbacks for a detached view or empty clamped range.
Exceptions
Visitorexceptions propagate unchanged.
std::string ownedText;
value.forEachChunk(FlowUi::TextRange{0, value.sizeBytes()}, [&ownedText](std::string_view chunk) { ownedText.append(chunk); });
