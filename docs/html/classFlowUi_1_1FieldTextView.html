Detailed Description

Non-owning access to manager-owned single-line or chunked text.

The view is invalidated by the next mutation of its field. Single-line text is guaranteed contiguous. Multiline callers explicitly copy a range or visit its chunks, avoiding an accidental full-document flatten per frame.
